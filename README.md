# Hi, I'm Risini Dinara Kumarasinghe

I am an undergraduate majoring in Electronic Engineering, specializing in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Moratuwa. My primary interests are in biosignal processing, neuroengineering, and embedded biomedical systems, with a focus on EEG-based analysis and real-time signal processing for assistive technologies.

I secured 2nd place island-wide in the GCE O/L examination among over 300000 candidates and ranked 73rd island-wide in the Physical Sciences stream at the GCE A/L examination. I was the top-performing student in my school with the highest results in Combined Mathematics and Physics.

From December 2024 to July 2025, I worked at the AID Lab, University of Sydney on electrotactile stimulation systems, focusing on waveform generation and real-time multi-electrode control using precise PWM timing. I also conducted impedance and capacitance measurements on mycelium-based conductive composites and explored ear-EEG signal acquisition, including sensor placement, signal conditioning, and safety considerations.

I am currently working on a final-year project developing an OpenBCI-enabled Brain-Computer Interface for pediatric neurorehabilitation. The project focuses on non-invasive EEG signal acquisition, preprocessing, artifact mitigation, feature extraction, and evaluation of paradigms such as SSVEP, with integration into interactive game-based interfaces. The system is designed as a scalable research framework for broader neurorehabilitation applications.

Outside of academics, I enjoy spending time caring for animals.
